Privacy

What this site does and doesn't collect.

Plain starter text — have it reviewed before launch. Not legal advice.

The short version

This site does not track you. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising — and we never sell, share, or trade any data, because we don’t collect any to begin with.

What happens when you check a message

When you use the checker, your answers stay in your browser. Nothing you tap or type is sent to a server, and nothing is saved after you close the page. The verdict is worked out entirely on your own device.

Server logs at our hosting provider

Like nearly every website, the company that hosts this site (Cloudflare) may automatically record basic technical logs when pages are requested — such as your IP address, browser type, and the time of the request. These passive logs are created by the host to run and protect the service; we don’t use them to identify or profile you. Cloudflare’s own privacy policy governs its handling of that data.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask you to sign in or create an account.
  • We don’t set cookies or use tracking pixels.
  • We don’t run third-party analytics or advertising.
  • We don’t store the messages or details you’re worried about.
  • We don’t sell or share personal information with anyone.

California and state privacy notes

Because we don’t collect, sell, or share personal information, there is nothing to opt out of under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or similar state laws. For California’s online-privacy disclosure law (CalOPPA): we don’t track visitors over time or across sites, and we don’t respond to “Do Not Track” signals because there is no tracking to switch off.

Children

This site is intended for adults and for teens 13 and older; it is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since the site collects no personal data from anyone, no child-specific data is collected either.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to outside organizations, like the FTC or your bank, so you can report a scam or verify a message. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don’t control.

Questions

If you have a question about privacy on this site, please reach out through the contact method listed at launch.
