About this site

An independent, free, public-service project to help people check suspicious calls, texts, and messages.

This site is an independent public-service consumer-safety project. It exists to do one thing: help a worried person slow down, check a suspicious call, text, or message against the warning signs scammers rely on, and find the right official place to verify or report it.

What this site is not. It is not a government site, and it is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any company or agency named on it. Company and agency names appear only to describe who scammers impersonate. The checker gives general information and an automated read of warning signs — not legal or financial advice, and never a guarantee.

How it’s run. The site is free, has no accounts, sets no cookies, and runs no trackers. Anything you type stays in your browser. You can read more in the privacy policy.

How it’s funded — and what we don’t do. This is a self-funded, public-service project. It runs no advertising and no affiliate links, so nothing here is trying to sell you a product or steer you toward a paid service. There is nobody to pay and nothing to buy. The front-end code that runs this site is open-source and anyone can inspect it on GitHub, so you don’t have to take our word for how it works.

If a contact or detail on this site looks out of date, please check the official website it points to — and trust the official site over us.
