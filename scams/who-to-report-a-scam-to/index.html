Who to report a scam to

A US directory of where to report scams — agencies, carriers, platforms, and support lines.

Reporting a scam can help investigators stop it and warn others. You don’t need every detail to file a report. Match the report to the agency that fits — and if you lost money or shared information, start with What to do if you paid or shared information first.

Please double-check every contact below before you use it. These details haven't been independently verified — look each one up on its official website, and type the address yourself rather than tapping a link.
